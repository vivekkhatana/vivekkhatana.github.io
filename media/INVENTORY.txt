raw files or alternate copies without a delivery reason. Preserve originals locally
before compression and inspect technical labels/axes after optimization.

CURRENT SUPPORT AND FUTURE CAROUSEL
Existing HTML/CSS/JS supports multiple marked native images and videos under each
project, intrinsic aspect ratios, figure captions, video posters, native playback
controls, click-to-play/preload=none, pausing and error fallbacks. Actual cards
currently retain their existing development placeholders; this setup changes no UI.
Carousel controls, thumbnail navigation and image fullscreen UI are NOT implemented
by this directory-only step. Native video fullscreen depends on browser/platform.
When multiple approved assets arrive, prepare one compact labeled carousel per
project with previous/next buttons, slide captions and optional labeled thumbnails.
Keep it manually navigable by keyboard, with a readable no-JS list of media; avoid
automatic slide changes. Pause videos when their slide is hidden. Preserve natural
image/video proportions with no figure cropping, and offer fullscreen where useful.
No audio autoplay or eager download of all videos. Retain native video controls
and visible fallbacks/external links. Respect reduced motion and preserve card styling.
