About

Bio

I develop mathematical and algorithmic foundations for intelligent, safe, and resilient physical systems, drawing on distributed optimization, control theory, and machine learning. My research seeks to enable autonomous systems to reason, learn, and coordinate under uncertainty while maintaining verifiable safety, feasibility, and performance guarantees. Applications of my work span robotics, autonomous mobility, energy systems, and critical infrastructure.

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Nevada, Reno, and a Research Affiliate at the Center for Autonomous Vehicles in Air Transportation Engineering (AVIATE) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, working with Profs. Naira Hovakimyan, Petros Voulgaris, and Srinivasa Salapaka.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering with a minor in Mathematics from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities in 2025, advised by Prof. Murti V. Salapaka. My thesis is titled Balancing Information Mixing and Optimality: A Framework for Robust and Efficient Distributed Decision-Making. I also received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Minnesota in 2020, and my B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee in 2018. I received the Distinguished Dissertation Award (Honorable Mention) from the University of Minnesota in 2026. I have also held research positions at the National Laboratory of the Rockies (2023) and the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (2017).

I am an active member of the IEEE Control Systems Society, the IEEE Power & Energy Society, the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, the INFORMS Optimization Society, and the INFORMS Decision Analysis Society. I am also a Volunteer Member of the National Postdoctoral Association. Outside the lab, I enjoy reading, art shows, sports, hiking, and honing my culinary skills.

Research Vision

Research

My research asks how interconnected physical systems can learn, coordinate, and act reliably under uncertainty. I combine distributed optimization, control theory, and machine learning to study verifiable safety, feasibility, and performance across robotics, autonomous mobility, energy systems, and critical infrastructure.

⚙️

Distributed Optimization and Networked Intelligence

I study how agents coordinate decisions over directed networks with limited communication. My work spans consensus, distributed and decentralized optimization, and networked control, including nonconvex, difference-of-convex, and federated learning formulations and finite-time feasibility and optimality certificates.

🛡️

Safe and Adaptive Autonomy Under Uncertainty

I study how autonomous systems maintain safety as uncertainty and models change. My work combines stochastic, distributionally robust, model predictive, and adaptive control, including Wasserstein ambiguity sets and L1-adaptive architectures.

🤖

Learning-Enabled Autonomy and Collective Physical AI

I study how learning supports reliable collective autonomy. My work connects data-driven modeling, online learning, and learning–control co-design to the coordination of heterogeneous agents in robotics and autonomous mobility.

⚡

Resilient Energy and Critical Infrastructure

I study how interconnected infrastructure sustains critical services under uncertainty. My work addresses distributed control of microgrids and virtual power plants, storage planning, risk sharing, and resilient coordination of distributed energy resources.
