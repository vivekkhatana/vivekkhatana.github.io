About

Bio

I develop algorithms for distributed decision-making in networked systems — bridging control theory, optimization, and machine learning toward resilient autonomous systems.

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Center for Autonomous Vehicles in Air Transportation Engineering (AVIATE) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Nevada Reno, working with Profs. Petros Voulgaris, Naira Hovakimyan, and Srinivasa Salapaka.

I obtained my Ph.D from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities working with Prof. Murti Salapaka at the Nanodynamics Laboratory. My thesis focused on advancing distributed decision-making by balancing information mixing and optimality in distributed optimization — developing systems that ensure resilient distributed operation while preserving the efficiencies of centralized architectures.

I am an active member of the IEEE Control Systems Society, the IEEE Power and Energy Society, and the INFORMS Optimization Society. Outside research, I enjoy reading, art shows, sports, hiking, and honing my culinary skills.

Curriculum Vitae

Vivek Khatana is currently a Postdoctoral Scholar at the AVIATE Center, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and the Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Nevada Reno. His research focuses on optimization, stochastic control, networks of dynamical systems, data-driven system identification, and power systems control.

He obtained his Ph.D from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities and his B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. He was recognized as an Outstanding Teaching Assistant at UMN (2021) and received the Summer Undergraduate Research Award at IIT Roorkee (2016).

Focus Areas

Research Interests

My research synergistically combines control theory and optimization to advance renewable energy adoption and autonomous systems. Current focus areas:

⚙️
Distributed Nonconvex Optimization
Algorithms for multi-agent systems with non-convex objectives and communication constraints
🧠
Stochastic Optimization for ML
Scalable stochastic methods for training deep and machine learning models
✈️
Advanced Aerospace Control
Control design for autonomous vehicles and emerging aerospace platforms
⚡
Distributed Energy Systems
Aggregation, coordination, and control of distributed energy resources and microgrids
