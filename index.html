About

Bio

I build the mathematical and algorithmic foundations for scalable, resilient, and certifiably safe autonomy — uniting distributed optimization, control theory, and machine learning to tackle real-world challenges in energy systems, robotics, and autonomous mobility.

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at the Center for Autonomous Vehicles in Air Transportation Engineering (AVIATE) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Nevada Reno, working with Profs. Naira Hovakimyan, Petros Voulgaris, and Srinivasa Salapaka.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering (minor in Mathematics) from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities in 2025, advised by Prof. Murti V. Salapaka. My thesis, Balancing Information Mixing and Optimality: A Framework for Robust and Efficient Distributed Decision-Making, was the first to establish finite-time optimality and feasibility certificates for constrained distributed optimization — closing a longstanding gap in the field. Prior to Minnesota, I received my B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee in 2018.

I have also held research positions at the National Renewable Energy Laboratory (2023) and the Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (2017). My work has been validated in real-world deployments at the Minnesota Fairview Medical Center and Minneapolis suburban power networks, achieving a 25% improvement in sustained critical-load capacity during outages. Great River Energy, a major power cooperative in Minnesota, is pursuing infrastructure investments informed by my research on interconnection thresholds for risk pooling.

I am an active member of the IEEE Control Systems Society, the IEEE Power and Energy Society, and the INFORMS Optimization Society. I currently serve as a Session Co-Chair at the 2026 IEEE American Control Conference and as a Volunteer Member of the National Postdoctoral Association. Outside the lab, I enjoy reading, art shows, sports, hiking, and honing my culinary skills.

Curriculum Vitae

Vivek Khatana is a Postdoctoral Scholar at the AVIATE Center, University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, and the Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Nevada Reno. His research advances resilient, scalable, and intelligent autonomy in complex interconnected systems, drawing on distributed optimization, control theory, and machine learning. His work spans energy systems, autonomous mobility, and robotics, combining rigorous theory with real-world experimental validation.

He received his Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Minnesota Twin Cities and his B.Tech. in Electrical Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. Focus Areas

Research Interests

My research addresses three pressing challenges in the next generation of intelligent infrastructures: how to make scalable decisions across large networks, how to ensure resilience and safety under uncertainty, and how to achieve adaptivity through learning from data. I pursue these through a combination of theoretical foundations, algorithmic innovation, and real-world experimental validation across energy systems, robotics, and autonomous mobility.

⚙️
Distributed Nonconvex Optimization
Finite-time feasible and optimal algorithms for constrained multi-agent systems over directed networks, including difference-of-convex and federated learning formulations
🛡️
Safe & Predictable Autonomy
Distributionally robust stochastic control with Wasserstein ambiguity sets; L1-adaptive architectures for certifiable safety under uncertainty in physical AI systems
⚡
Power & Energy Systems
Distributed control and optimization of microgrids and virtual power plants; storage planning and risk pooling for resilient distributed energy resource coordination
🤖
Learning-Enabled Autonomous Systems
Coordination of learning-based and model-based agents for robotics, urban air mobility, and data-driven networked control under limited communication
